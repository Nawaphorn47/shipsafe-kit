Last updated: October 9, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If a product isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you in full.

After a refund

Your license to the product ends, and we ask that you delete your copy.

Exceptions

We may decline refunds after 14 days, or for repeated purchase-and-refund patterns. If a product doesn't work because of a problem on our side, contact us at any time and we will fix it, replace it or refund you.
