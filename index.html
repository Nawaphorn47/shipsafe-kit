Building with AI is fast. Shipping is still hard.

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Cursor will happily write thousands of lines for you. What they won't do is keep track of what you're building, or tell you it's not safe to launch yet.

  • You start a new chat and the AI has forgotten every decision you made last week.
  • The same bug comes back because nobody wrote down why it happened.
  • You launch, then find out the database was readable by anyone with the public key.
  • After launch you have no idea what users want, so you build the wrong next feature.
